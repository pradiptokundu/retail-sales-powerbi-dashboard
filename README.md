# Retail Sales Analytics — Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze retail sales performance across products, customers, regions, categories, and business segments.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data cleaning, data modeling, DAX calculations, time-intelligence analysis, interactive filtering, drill-through analysis, and business insight generation.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw retail transaction data into an interactive management dashboard that helps answer questions such as:

* How are overall sales and profitability performing?
* Which regions and categories generate the most revenue?
* Which products contribute the most sales and profit?
* Which customers generate the highest value?
* Where are potential profitability and pricing opportunities?
* How does business performance change over time?

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights several business patterns, including:

* The Consumer segment contributes the largest share of sales.
* The West region is the strongest overall sales-performing region.
* Technology products are among the strongest contributors to revenue.
* Several products and sub-categories generate strong sales but comparatively lower profitability.
* Revenue and profit are concentrated among a relatively small group of high-performing products and customers.
* Regional and customer-level performance varies significantly, creating opportunities for targeted pricing, inventory, and marketing strategies.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

* Review pricing strategies for high-sales, low-profit products.
* Optimize discount policies where discounting may be affecting profitability.
* Strengthen inventory planning for consistently high-performing products.
* Replicate successful regional sales strategies across lower-performing regions.
* Develop targeted customer retention and loyalty strategies for high-value customers.
* Continue monitoring product and regional performance through periodic dashboard reviews.
